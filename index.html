What is this tool for?

This tool prepares the strategy and instructions for an AI-assisted presentation. It does not ask you to upload your report here and it does not create a PowerPoint file.

Recommended prompt size: approximately 450 to 750 words before your own answers. This is enough to control the workflow without competing with a long PDF or report.

The strategy remains strong

The builder keeps BLUF, SCQA, Minto Pyramid, and Golden Thread. You answer straightforward questions; the generated prompt applies the presentation methods.

BLUF
Lead with the conclusion or decision.
SCQA
Frame the situation, complication, question, and answer.
Minto
Group the supporting case under the main answer.
Golden Thread
Keep every main slide tied to one central question.
