# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors should communicate respectfully, focus on the work, welcome constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be clear, professional, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Do not post meeting records, personal information, credentials, or confidential material.
- Help maintain an inclusive and productive project environment.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Threats or personal attacks
- Publishing another person's private information
- Deliberate disruption or spam
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The owner may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
